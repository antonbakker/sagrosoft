KPI Definitions

KPICalculation
Hours (employee/equipment)Sum of all entries. Equipment hours = entries where equipmentId is not null.
Costs (employee/equipment)From TimesheetArticleEntry.calculatedAmount. Split by whether entry has equipmentId.
Own / IC hoursOwn = project.divisionId == timesheet.divisionId. IC = different.
Own / IC costsSame split applied to calculatedAmount.
ResourcesCount distinct employeeIds / count distinct equipmentIds in filtered set.
PendingCount of timesheets with status = SUBMITTED in current filter.

Intercompany (IC) Logic

IC = project.divisionId ≠ timesheet.divisionId (which equals the employee's division).

  • IC entries get amber left-border + [IC] badge
  • In "Group by Division": IC section shows where employees went (outgoing) and who came in (incoming)
  • Equipment on IC projects is included in the IC cost/hours calculation
  • KPI "Own / IC" uses this split for both hours and costs

Costs Source

Costs come from TimesheetArticleEntry.calculatedAmount (pre-resolved by CAO calculator at submission time). If no article entries exist (e.g. old migrated data), cost shows "—". Equipment costs are the subset of article entries linked to equipment-bearing timesheet entries.
